Add a CanvasFrame TV
Turn on debugging

  1. On the TV: Settings → System → About, select Android TV OS build seven times until it says you're a developer.
  2. Back in Settings → System → Developer options, turn on USB debugging.
  3. The TV then asks "Allow USB debugging?" for CanvasFrame: tick Always allow from this computer and choose Allow.

CanvasFrame uses this only inside the TV, to switch inputs, change the picture and press keys. Nothing outside the TV connects to it.
