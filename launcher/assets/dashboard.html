⚠ You could be locked out

To sign back in you'll need a 6-digit code from your authenticator app (). If you don't have that app any more, you won't be able to get back into this dashboard.

The authenticator is shared by all your linked TVs, so losing it locks you out of every CanvasFrame dashboard.

Recovery needs the PC: A reset turns sign-in off (and removes the authenticators) on every linked TV.
Approve the dashboard on the TV. A "Allow USB debugging?" prompt should be showing on the TV. Tick Always allow from this computer and choose Allow. Until then, the remote and app controls won't work.

CanvasFrame for Windows

CanvasFrame Link A small app for your Windows PC: all your CanvasFrame TVs in the tray and in a window like this one, and, if the PC uses a TV as its monitor, the TV switches to the PC (with its picture mode) when the PC wakes and back to CanvasFrame when its display turns off. This download already knows this TV, so adding it takes one click.
Download

Run it once: it installs itself for your Windows account (Start menu, Settings → Apps), no administrator rights needed. It isn't signed by a publisher, so Windows may warn first: choose More info → Run anyway (in Edge's download list: … → Keep). Windows 10 or 11.

Screen saver

Dim at nightHow much dimmer CanvasFrame's screen saver gets after dark (half as much at dawn and dusk), using sunrise and sunset from the weather location. It lowers the TV's backlight, saving power, and puts yours back when the screen saver ends; where a TV doesn't allow that, it darkens the picture instead.
Then fade to minimum afterAt night, once the screen saver has been on this long, it slowly fades the rest of the way to the lowest backlight over about 5 minutes. The picture stays faintly visible; any key brings your normal backlight back.

Dimming

Dim by room lightUses the TV's own light sensor instead of the clock: the darker the room, the dimmer CanvasFrame gets (up to the "Dim at night" amount), and the late fade starts once the room is dark. The sensor only runs while LG's AI Brightness is on, so this switches it on (LG then also adjusts the picture to the room while you watch) and back off again if CanvasFrame turned it on.
Light fromWhere the room light comes from. Another TV's sensor works when that TV shares it (an LG with AI Brightness on). If the chosen source has no reading, dimming follows the clock.
Brighter or darkerShifts the sensor reading: towards Brighter, CanvasFrame treats the room as lighter than measured and dims less; towards Darker, it dims more.

Consistency

Rooms

Rooms marked "next to" each other count as nearby. When someone uses a TV (remote, turning it on, waking its screen saver), CanvasFrame screen savers in the same room act as if they'd been touched: the late-night fade starts over. In a room next door it's gentler: fading is delayed and a deep fade lifts partway.
